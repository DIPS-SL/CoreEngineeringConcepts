Why this system exists

A phone on a strong connection can happily pull down a 4K file. A phone on one bar of 4G can't — and it shouldn't have to. If a service only ever stores and serves a single master rendition, every low-bandwidth viewer either stalls out or can't watch at all. A transcoding pipeline fixes this by producing several smaller renditions ahead of time, so each device gets handed the one that actually fits its connection. That's the problem the next two tabs solve.
