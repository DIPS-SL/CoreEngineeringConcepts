The Fix — ML Scoring Solution
Every candidate post gets a relevancy score. Highest scores surface first — regardless of time.
📐 Ranking Score Formula
score = ( 0.35 × engagement_rate -- likes + comments + shares + 0.25 × affinity_score -- how often user interacts with author + 0.20 × recency_decay -- e^(-λt) — older posts score lower + 0.15 × content_relevance -- topic match from user interest model + 0.05 × diversity_boost -- avoid repeating the same author )

⚡
50,000 posts ranked in ~100ms
The key is that the expensive ML step only runs on 500 pre-filtered candidates, not all 50k. Each stage aggressively reduces the candidate pool — and every stage runs in parallel across a model serving cluster.

🔑
Implicit signals beat explicit ones at scale
A like is binary. Watch time, scroll depth, and hover duration reveal genuine interest that users never consciously express. TikTok's dominance is built on optimising watch time — an implicit signal — not likes. The best ranking models weight implicit signals heavily.
🎙
Key Takeaways
What every engineer should know about relevancy and ranking.
⏱
Chrono first
Start with chronological. Simple to build, easy to debug, zero ML overhead. Move to ranking only when content volume overwhelms users.
🧠
ML Scoring
Each candidate post gets a weighted score: engagement rate, affinity, recency decay, content relevance, diversity. Highest score wins position.
⚙️
4-Stage Pipeline
Retrieve → Filter → Score → Re-rank. Aggressive funnel: 50k candidates down to 20 in ~100ms. Filter early to reduce ML inference cost.
📡
Implicit Signals
Watch time, scroll depth, hover — these are harder to fake and reveal true engagement. TikTok's moat is built on implicit signal optimisation.
💡
The rule of thumb
Ranking is a quality problem, not a speed problem. A chronological feed loads instantly but buries your best content under noise. ML scoring inverts that — but the scoring pipeline must itself be fast. If your ML inference takes 500ms, your users feel it. The goal: maximum relevance, under 150ms, at every scale.
